# Warehouse modernisation

**Client:** Orbitwave Retail (synthetic) (logistics) | **Duration:** 13 weeks | **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client came to me because analysts queried a fragile collection of CSV exports.

## What I did
- Used ETL for the core of the solution.
- Kept version-controlled SQL transformation models with tests and lineage docs.
- Ran the warehouse on an elastic cloud data platform.
- Wrote window-function queries over millions of rows.

## Result
The project cut dashboard refresh time by 50% within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Add monitoring from day one.
