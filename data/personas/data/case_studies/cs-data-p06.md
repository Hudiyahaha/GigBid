# Customer segmentation study

**Client:** Brightpoint Group (synthetic) (retail) | **Duration:** 8 weeks | **Year:** 2026

## The problem
The client came to me because a retailer sent the same email to every customer.

## What I did

- Fitted statistical models in a language built for statisticians.
