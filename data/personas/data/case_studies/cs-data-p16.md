# Warehouse modernisation for a logistics client

**Client:** Brightwave Media (synthetic) (logistics) | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client came to me because analysts queried a fragile collection of CSV exports.

## What I did
- Made charts that explained the numbers at a glance, using Data visualization.
- Kept version-controlled SQL transformation models with tests and lineage docs.
- Ran the warehouse on an elastic cloud data platform.
- Moved and cleaned data from source systems into one store every night.

## Result
The project cut dashboard refresh time by 26% within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Add monitoring from day one.
