# Customer segmentation study for a real estate client

**Client:** Peakwave Health (synthetic) (real estate) | **Duration:** 7 weeks | **Year:** 2022

## The problem
The client came to me because a retailer sent the same email to every customer.

## What I did
- Ran regressions and hypothesis tests with confidence intervals, using Statistics.
- Wrote window-function queries over millions of rows, using SQL.
- Kept version-controlled SQL transformation models with tests and lineage docs, using dbt.
- Fitted statistical models in a language built for statisticians.

## Result
The project lifted email revenue 62% using segments within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Add monitoring from day one.
