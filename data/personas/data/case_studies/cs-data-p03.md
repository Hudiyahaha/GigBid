# Pricing experiment analysis

**Client:** Northgate Learning (synthetic) (manufacturing) | **Duration:** 14 weeks | **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client came to me because an app changed prices without knowing if it helped.

## What I did
- Designed controlled experiments and computed significance and power, using A/B testing.
- Used pandas for the core of the solution.
- Ran regressions and hypothesis tests with confidence intervals.
- Scripted the whole pipeline end to end.

## Result
The project found a price that lifted conversion 30% within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Write the runbook earlier.
