# Marketing attribution model

**Client:** Emberleaf Learning (synthetic) (manufacturing) | **Duration:** 10 weeks | **Year:** 2022

## The problem
The client came to me because a marketing team could not tell which channel drove sales.

## What I did
- Wrote window-function queries over millions of rows, using SQL.
- Used Looker Studio for the core of the solution.
- Instrumented website events and conversion funnels.
- Queried event data in a serverless columnar warehouse.

## Result
The project re-allocated 27% of ad spend to better channels within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Write the runbook earlier.
