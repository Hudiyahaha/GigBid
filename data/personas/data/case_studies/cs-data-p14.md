# CRM data clean-up v2 (healthcare)

**Client:** Clearforge Media (synthetic) (healthcare) | **Duration:** 14 weeks | **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client came to me because a sales team's CRM had thousands of duplicate contacts.

## What I did
- Deduplicated and standardised inconsistent records, using Data cleaning.
- Scripted the whole pipeline end to end.
- Built pivot-table workbooks with lookups for the finance team.
- Fitted statistical models in a language built for statisticians.

## Result
The project merged 12000 duplicate records within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Write the runbook earlier.
