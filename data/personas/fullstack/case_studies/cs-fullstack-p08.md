# Clinic booking platform v2 (logistics)

**Client:** Greenline Foods (synthetic) (logistics) | **Duration:** 13 weeks | **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client came to me because patients phoned the clinic to book and reschedule appointments.

## What I did
- Used file-based routing with server-side rendering, using Next.js.
- Kept the whole codebase strictly typed, using TypeScript.
- Handled sign-in, sessions and password resets securely, using Authentication.
- Generated a type-safe ORM client from a schema file.
- Cached hot reads in an in-memory key-value store.

## Result
The project moved 47% of bookings online within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Agree on evaluation metrics before building.
