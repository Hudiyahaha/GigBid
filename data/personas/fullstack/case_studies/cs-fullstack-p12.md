# Fitness tracking PWA v2 (non-profit)

**Client:** Summitwave Studio (synthetic) (non-profit) | **Duration:** 10 weeks | **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client came to me because a gym wanted members to log workouts from their phones.

## What I did
- Used a hosted backend for auth and realtime data, using Firebase.
- Used Authentication for the core of the solution.
- Styled everything with utility classes.
- Ran lint, tests and deploys automatically on every pull request.

## Result
The project reached 1200 weekly active users within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Involve end users in week one.
