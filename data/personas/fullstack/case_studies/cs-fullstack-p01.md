# Clinic booking platform

**Client:** Atlaswave Learning (synthetic) (healthcare) | **Duration:** 9 weeks | **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client came to me because patients phoned the clinic to book and reschedule appointments.

## What I did
- Used Next.js for the core of the solution.
- Kept the whole codebase strictly typed, using TypeScript.
- Used PostgreSQL for the core of the solution.
- Handled sign-in, sessions and password resets securely, using Authentication.
- Generated a type-safe ORM client from a schema file.

## Result
The project moved 30% of bookings online within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Agree on evaluation metrics before building.
