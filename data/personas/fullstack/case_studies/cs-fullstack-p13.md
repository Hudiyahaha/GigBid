# E2E test and CI overhaul v2 (travel)

**Client:** Novawave Media (synthetic) (travel) | **Duration:** 13 weeks | **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client came to me because a SaaS team shipped regressions every release.

## What I did
- Used TypeScript for the core of the solution.
- Covered the critical user journeys with browser end-to-end tests.
- Packaged everything into reproducible container images.
- Handled sign-in, sessions and password resets securely.

## Result
The project cut escaped bugs by 64% within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Write the runbook earlier.
