# Fitness tracking PWA

**Client:** Brightfield Studio (synthetic) (media) | **Duration:** 9 weeks | **Year:** 2026

## The problem
The client came to me because a gym wanted members to log workouts from their phones.

## What I did
- Used a hosted backend for auth and realtime data, using Firebase.
- Built the interface from reusable stateful components with hooks.
- Styled everything with utility classes.
- Handled sign-in, sessions and password resets securely.

## Result
The project reached 1200 weekly active users within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Add monitoring from day one.
