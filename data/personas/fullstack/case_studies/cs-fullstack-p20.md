# E2E test and CI overhaul for a SaaS client

**Client:** Peakwave Retail (synthetic) (SaaS) | **Duration:** 6 weeks | **Year:** 2026

## The problem
The client came to me because a SaaS team shipped regressions every release.

## What I did
- Used Playwright for the core of the solution.
- Kept the whole codebase strictly typed, using TypeScript.
- Ran lint, tests and deploys automatically on every pull request.
- Structured the backend in modules with dependency injection.

## Result
The project cut escaped bugs by 18% within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Involve end users in week one.
