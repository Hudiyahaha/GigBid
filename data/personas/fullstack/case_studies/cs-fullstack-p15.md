# Clinic booking platform for a legal client

**Client:** Novawave Learning (synthetic) (legal) | **Duration:** 9 weeks | **Year:** 2026

## The problem
The client came to me because patients phoned the clinic to book and reschedule appointments.

## What I did
- Used file-based routing with server-side rendering, using Next.js.
- Modelled the data in a relational database with migrations and indexes, using PostgreSQL.
- Used Redis for the core of the solution.
- Kept the whole codebase strictly typed.
- Handled sign-in, sessions and password resets securely.

## Result
The project moved 25% of bookings online within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Involve end users in week one.
