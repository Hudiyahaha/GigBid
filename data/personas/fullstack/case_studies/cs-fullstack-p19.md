# Fitness tracking PWA for a education client

**Client:** Bluewave Systems (synthetic) (education) | **Duration:** 8 weeks | **Year:** 2025

## The problem
The client came to me because a gym wanted members to log workouts from their phones.

## What I did
- Used Firebase for the core of the solution.
- Cached hot reads in an in-memory key-value store, using Redis.
- Built the interface from reusable stateful components with hooks.
- Styled everything with utility classes.

## Result
The project reached 4000 weekly active users within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Agree on evaluation metrics before building.
