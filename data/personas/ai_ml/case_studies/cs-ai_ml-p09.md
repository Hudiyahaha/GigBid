# Customer churn model v2 (education)

**Client:** Ironpath Foods (synthetic) (education) | **Duration:** 3 weeks | **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client came to me because a subscription business could not see which customers were about to leave.

## What I did
- Compared gradient-free baselines with cross-validated pipelines, using scikit-learn.
- Used Model evaluation for the core of the solution.
- Tracked every experiment's parameters, metrics and model artefacts.
- Scripted the whole pipeline end to end.
- Used boosted decision trees tuned with early stopping.

## Result
The project raised recall on churners to 0.54 within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Involve end users in week one.
