# Clinical note entity extractor

**Client:** Greenline Systems (synthetic) (non-profit) | **Duration:** 16 weeks | **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client came to me because a health-tech team needed medications pulled out of free-text notes.

## What I did
- Extracted named entities with an industrial NLP pipeline, using spaCy.
- Fine-tuned a pretrained encoder checkpoint from the model hub, using Hugging Face Transformers.
- Processed free-text customer messages at scale, using NLP.

## Result
The project achieved an F1 of 0.78 on held-out notes within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Agree on evaluation metrics before building.
