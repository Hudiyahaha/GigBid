# Customer churn model for a real estate client

**Client:** Lumenfield Ventures (synthetic) (real estate) | **Duration:** 4 weeks | **Year:** 2025

## The problem
The client came to me because a subscription business could not see which customers were about to leave.

## What I did
- Used scikit-learn for the core of the solution.
- Used boosted decision trees tuned with early stopping.
- Tracked every experiment's parameters, metrics and model artefacts.
- Scripted the whole pipeline end to end.
- Processed free-text customer messages at scale.

## Result
The project raised recall on churners to 0.43 within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Agree on evaluation metrics before building.
