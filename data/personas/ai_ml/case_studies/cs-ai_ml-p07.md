# Invoice data extraction pipeline

**Client:** Silverstone Group (synthetic) (gaming) | **Duration:** 12 weeks | **Year:** 2025

## The problem
The client came to me because an accounts team typed invoice fields into the ERP manually.

## What I did
- Called a hosted chat-completion model with JSON-mode outputs, using OpenAI API.
- Used Celery for the core of the solution.
- Used Python for the core of the solution.
- Used Docker for the core of the solution.

## Result
The project processed 2500 invoices a day unattended within the first months after launch.

## What I would do differently
Involve end users in week one.
