# Semantic product search v2 (retail)

**Client:** Summitwave Retail (synthetic) (retail) | **Duration:** 5 weeks | **Year:** 2025

## The problem
The client came to me because an online shop's keyword search missed obvious matches.

## What I did

- Grounded the assistant's answers in retrieved document passages with citations.
